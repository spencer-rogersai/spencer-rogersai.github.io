Before you open one

Market Explorer works immediately. The other two start as an empty map: click Get latest data inside the tool and it downloads the current file from the state, or load a file you have saved. Either way the data stays in your browser and is sent nowhere.

  • CCLD Transparency Website California Department of Social Services. Licensing files for residential care facilities and home care agencies, refreshed every few days.
  • Healthcare Facility Locations California Department of Public Health. Refreshed monthly; the DATA_DATE column shows the extract date.

Status

This is a staging index, not a release. Versions here may move without notice and the tools have not been through outside review. Figures on this page were measured against the September 2026 extracts and will drift as the source files are refreshed.
